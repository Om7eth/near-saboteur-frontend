The mechanic

How the token works

Four rules, all inside the token contract. The game only reports what happened in a match. The contract decides every amount.

01
Play to earn

Finish a match and the contract pays by rank: 1,000 / 600 / 400, 100 for finishing. Full rewards need 4+ real players, so bots can't farm it.

02
Catch to burn

The crew votes out the real saboteur and the contract burns 1,000 from its Ritual Pool. It never burns from players.

03
Burn for Play Pass

Burn 15,000 $SABOTEUR to unlock Play Pass forever. Premium skins, stickers, a gold name and 2× rewards.

04
Burns grow rewards

Next week's reward budget is 150,000 plus 50% of this week's burns. More real games mean more catches and bigger rewards.

Rules in the contract

Fixed at launch and readable by anyone with get_rules.

No one can change these rules quietly, not the game and not the owner. A change is proposed in public and applies only after 7 days, inside hard limits written in the contract. Each match id is accepted once, so nothing is ever paid or burned twice.
