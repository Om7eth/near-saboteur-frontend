01Get started

From opening the game to your first match.

1 · Create a free account — or jump in as a guest

Tap PLAY NOW to make a free account (takes under a minute) or jump in as a guest to try it first. An account keeps your points, rank, skins and wallet.

2 · Your hangar

This is home. Change your crew skin with the arrows under your character, see live rooms on the left and this week's ranks on the right.

Pick a mode at the bottom — FREE, POOL or PAID — and press the big button.

3 · Host a room or join one

Host: name your room, choose how many matches and make it public or private.
Join: pick an open room, or type a friend's 4-letter room code.

4 · Fill the lobby (bots welcome)

You need 4+ players. Short on friends? The host taps HOST → + ADD BOT. Bots do missions, can be the Saboteur and even argue in meetings.

The host also sets the number of Saboteurs (1–3) and the game speed, then presses START.

03Play as Crew

Do your missions, watch everyone, survive.

Your missions

Tap MISSIONS to see your 5 tasks and which room each one is in. Every mission you finish fills the shared CREW TASKS bar.

When the bar reaches 100%, the Crew wins.

Find your way

Press M (or the map button) for the station map. 14 rooms are joined by corridors — the dot is you.

Walk up and press E

Stand next to a glowing station and a prompt appears. Press E (or tap it) to start that mission.

Missions are real NEAR things

Stake NEAR, do a cross-chain swap, repair the power grid… In the NEAR AI mission you chat with the real NEAR AI — ask it anything, 3 questions to finish.

Seen a body? Press R to report it — that starts a meeting right away. No body but something's fishy? Go to the Assembly Hall and press the red Call Assembly button.

04Play as Saboteur

Strike when nobody's watching. Then act innocent.

Eliminate

Get close to a crewmate and the red KILL button lights up — press Q or tap it. It has a cooldown, so choose your moment. Make sure nobody else is nearby.

Sabotage

Press F for your tools: cut the electricity, trigger a hacker attack, lock gates. Using one locks all of them for 20 seconds. Use vents to escape fast.

What the Crew sees

Cut Electricity makes everyone's view go dark, and an alert points to the problem room. Crew have to go and fix it — perfect cover for you.

You can fake missions too. Stand at stations, follow groups, and have a story ready for the meeting.

05Meetings & voting

This is where the game is won.

1 · The alarm

A body report or the Assembly button stops the match and pulls everyone into a meeting.

2 · Discuss (2.5 min)

Say where you were and what you saw. Ask questions, call out lies, @tag someone to ask them directly. Bots answer too.

If 60% press Skip to vote, voting starts early.

3 · Vote (30 s)

Tap a player, then confirm — or Skip vote if you're not sure. One vote each, and the chat stays open while you vote.

4 · The verdict

The player with the most votes is ejected and their role is revealed. A tie or a skip-win means nobody leaves.

Died? You're a ghost

Ghosts can't talk to the living, but they keep watching and chat with other ghosts in the Ghosts tab. Crew ghosts can still finish missions.

07Game modes

FREE

Free Play

No wallet, no money. Host or join a room and play with friends or bots.

POOL

Pool Games

A host puts up a prize. Players join free, play 1–5 matches, and the top players by total points win it. Test NEAR paid automatically by the contract — or real NEAR, paid by the host in one click.

PAID

Paid Games

Everyone pays the same entry fee (testnet). The top 3 split the pot 50 / 30 / 20 after a 5% fee. Ties share their places.

09Quick answers

Do I need crypto to play?

No. Free Play needs nothing — not even an account. Wallets only matter for Pool/Paid games and Play Pass.

I got disconnected — is my match gone?

No. Reopen the game and you'll be put back in your seat automatically.

Who are the BOT players?

AI players powered by NEAR AI. They do missions, can be the Saboteur, and talk in meetings. The host can add or remove them.

Is $SABOTEUR worth money?

No. It's a testnet token with no value today, and nothing is promised about the future.
