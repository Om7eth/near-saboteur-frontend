01Get started

From opening the game to your first match.

1 · Create a free account — or jump in as a guest

Tap PLAY NOW to make a free account (takes under a minute) or jump in as a guest to try it first. An account keeps your points, rank, skins and wallet.

2 · Your hangar

This is home. Change your crew skin with the arrows under your character, see live rooms on the left and this week's ranks on the right.

Pick a mode at the bottom — FREE, POOL or PAID — and press the big button.

3 · Host a room or join one

Host: name your room, choose how many matches and make it public or private.
Join: pick an open room, or type a friend's 4-letter room code.

4 · Fill the lobby (bots welcome)

You need 4+ players. Short on friends? The host taps HOST → + ADD BOT. Bots do missions, can be the Saboteur and even argue in meetings.

The host also sets the number of Saboteurs (1–3) and the game speed, then presses START.

03Play as Crew

Do your missions, watch everyone, survive.

Your missions

Tap MISSIONS to see your 5 tasks and which room each one is in. Every mission you finish fills the shared CREW TASKS bar. A quick hint shows your Missions button at the start of every match.

When the bar reaches 100%, the Crew wins.

Find your way

The location card in the top-left shows the room you're in, a small map that slides as you walk, your mission rooms in gold and the route to them.

Tap the card (or press M) for the full station map — a floating window you can drag. Pick a mission on the right and it draws the walking route. Press ✕ to close it.

Walk up and press E

Stand next to a glowing station and a prompt appears. Press E (or tap it) to start that mission.

Missions are real NEAR things

Stake NEAR, do a cross-chain swap, repair the power grid… In the NEAR AI mission you chat with the real NEAR AI — ask it anything, 3 questions to finish.

Seen a body? Press R to report it — that starts a meeting right away. No body but something's fishy? Go to the Assembly Hall and press the red Call Assembly button.

04Play as Saboteur

Strike when nobody's watching. Then act innocent.

Eliminate

Get close to a crewmate and the red KILL button lights up — press Q or tap it. It has a cooldown, so choose your moment. Make sure nobody else is nearby.

Sabotage

Press F for your tools: cut the electricity, trigger a hacker attack, lock gates. Using one locks all of them for 20 seconds. Use vents to escape fast.

What the Crew sees

Cut Electricity makes everyone's view go dark, and an alert points to the problem room. Arrows lead the Crew along the real walking route and say HERE · PRESS E when they arrive. While they rush to fix it — perfect cover for you.

You can fake missions too. Stand at stations, follow groups, and have a story ready for the meeting.

05Meetings & voting

This is where the game is won.

1 · The alarm

A body report or the Assembly button stops the match and pulls everyone into a meeting.

2 · Discuss (2.5 min)

Say where you were and what you saw. Ask questions, call out lies, @tag someone to ask them directly. Bots answer too. Play Pass holders can drop stickers and GIFs.

If 60% press Skip to vote, voting starts early.

3 · Vote (30 s)

Tap a player, then confirm — or Skip vote if you're not sure. One vote each, and the chat stays open while you vote.

4 · The verdict

The player with the most votes is ejected and their role is revealed. A tie or a skip-win means nobody leaves.

Died? You're a ghost

Ghosts can't talk to the living, but they keep watching and chat with other ghosts in the Ghosts tab. Crew ghosts can still finish missions.

07Game modes

FREE

Free Play

No wallet, no money. Host or join a room and play with friends or bots.

POOL

Pool Games

A host puts up a prize. Players join free, play 1–5 matches, and the top players by total points win it. Testnet: the contract pays automatically. Real NEAR: the host pays every winner from their own wallet.

PAID

Paid Games

Everyone pays the same entry fee (testnet). The top 3 split the pot 50 / 30 / 20 after a 5% fee. Ties share their places.

Real NEAR pools: paying winners

Players add their mainnet address (name.near, name.tg…) when they join. When the last match ends, the host opens Pool Games → My History → Pay winners and taps PAY ALL — one wallet approval pays everyone. Or send each amount yourself and press Mark as paid.

Every payment is checked on the NEAR blockchain and shows ✓ VERIFIED. The winner gets an Inbox message, and the home page reminds the host until everyone is paid. The game never holds real NEAR.

Host without playing: Watch mode

Hosting a pool for your community? Choose Watch and you spectate the whole match. Switch between players with the arrows (or A / D), or tap a name. You don't count as a player and you can't be killed.

08Weekly NEAR prizes

Top 3 every week win real NEAR

Open RANKS. The banner shows this week's prize and what #1, #2 and #3 win (in $ and NEAR). When the week resets — Saturday 00:00 IST — the top 3 are paid in real NEAR on mainnet, automatically, by the Saboteur Agent on NEAR Intents.

Add your mainnet wallet under Your prize wallet → Change on the Ranks page. No wallet yet? Your prize waits for you for 30 days. Ties go to whoever reached the score first. You get an Inbox message when you're queued and when you're paid.

Weekly prizes are optional and may change or stop at any time.

09Play Pass, wallet & ranks

  • Play Pass (one-time, real NEAR): premium skins, 24 chat stickers, GIFs, gold name and 2× $SABOTEUR.
  • Wallet: every account gets an in-game NEAR testnet wallet. For real NEAR (Play Pass, pools, prizes) you connect your own HOT, Meteor or Intear wallet.
  • Ranks: weekly and monthly leaderboards by points. The weekly top 3 win NEAR (see above).

10Referrals, Watch live & Telegram

REFERRALS

Invite friends

Open REFERRALS on the home screen and share your link (nearsaboteur.com/?ref=you). A friend signs up with it and finishes 3 matches with other real players → your reward unlocks 🔓: 1,000 $SABOTEUR + 30 points.

$SABOTEUR is a testnet token with no value, and nothing is promised about the future. Up to 5 friends a week add points to the weekly Ranks.

WATCH

Watch live matches

Tap 👀 Watch live matches on the home screen (or the Watch button on a Telegram post) to watch any public Free or Pool match. You see what the crew sees — no roles — and you can't chat or vote. Paid games can't be watched.

11Quick answers

Do I need crypto to play?

No. Free Play needs nothing — not even an account. Wallets only matter for Pool/Paid games and Play Pass.

I got disconnected — is my match gone?

No. Reopen the game and you'll be put back in your seat automatically.

Who are the BOT players?

AI players. They do missions, can be the Saboteur, and talk in meetings using NEAR AI (with simple backup lines if it's busy). The host can add or remove them.

How do I win the weekly NEAR prize?

Finish in the top 3 on the weekly Ranks and add a mainnet prize wallet on the Ranks page. The Saboteur Agent pays automatically after the Saturday reset.

Can people watching tell my friends who the Saboteur is?

Watchers never see roles and can't chat or vote in the game. They can still see what happens on screen (like a kill near the player they follow), so Paid games can't be watched at all.

Does the game hold my real NEAR?

No. Real-NEAR pools are paid by the host straight from their wallet, Play Pass and custom character requests go straight to saboteur.near, and weekly prizes come from the agent account. Paid games and testnet pools use test NEAR only.

Is $SABOTEUR worth money?

No. It's a testnet token with no value today, and nothing is promised about the future.
